Street = standard rate recorded at each move-in for that store, unit type and size, carried forward. Current tenants are exact; departed tenants are exact on each month-end rent roll, otherwise their rent between move-in and move-out is estimated (raises at anniversaries).

In-place rent vs. street

How current tenants' rent compares with the street rate for the same units, as a percentage. Above 0 (blue): tenants pay more than street, so when they move out and the unit re-rents at street, rent drops by about this much. Below 0 (orange): tenants pay less than street, so turnover raises rent. New tenants often start below street because of specials, so the real drop is usually a little bigger.
